# Design brief

> Status: **awaiting answers.** This document gets written from the questionnaire
> at `docs/design/questionnaire.html` once it's filled in.

The questionnaire is published as a private interactive page; answers are stored
with it and read back here. Sections it covers:

1. **The feeling** — mood words, the remembered moment, nearest neighbours, pressure level, solo vs. shared
2. **The place** — setting, scale, how time works, seasons and weather, local colour
3. **The neighbours** — species, how many, how you befriend them, whether they leave, how they talk
4. **Your days** — activities, the one that matters most, session length, economy shape, collecting
5. **The look and sound** — art direction, camera, palette, audio
6. **The build** — platform, tech, how hands-on the author is, first milestone, art source
7. **Making a character** — who the player is, what's customisable, creator depth, changing later, personality
8. **Anything else** — must-haves, never-haves, working title
